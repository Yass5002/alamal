Frequently Asked Questions

How do I prepare for blood tests?

Many blood tests require fasting for 8–12 hours prior to collection — no food or drinks except water. For specific tests like glucose tolerance tests or lipid profiles, special preparation may be needed. Always follow the instructions provided by your doctor or our laboratory staff.

How long does it take to get test results?

Most routine tests are available within 24–48 hours. Some specialized tests may take 3–7 days. For urgent tests, we offer expedited processing with results within a few hours. You can receive your results in person, by email, or through our secure online portal.

Do you offer home sample collection?

Yes, we provide home sample collection for patients who cannot visit our laboratory. This service is available 7 days a week. Please call us at 06 28 83 65 01 to schedule a home visit with our qualified phlebotomist.

Do I need an appointment for laboratory tests?

While appointments are not mandatory for routine tests, we recommend scheduling one to minimize waiting time. For specialized tests or during peak hours, appointments are advisable. You can schedule by phone or through our website.

Are your tests covered by insurance?

Most of our tests are covered by major health insurance providers in Morocco. We also work with CNOPS, CNSS, and private insurance companies. Please bring your insurance card and any necessary authorization forms; our staff will assist you with claims and documentation.
